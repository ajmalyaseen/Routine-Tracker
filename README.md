## 🛠️ Features Included

- ✅ **Interactive Tick Marks**: Mark subtasks done as you complete them with audio clicks.
- 📊 **Real-time Discipline Score**: Live progress bar (0% - 100%) and completed task tally.
- 🎉 **Celebration Confetti**: Triggers celebration animations at 50% and 100% milestone completion.
- 🔥 **Day Streak Counter**: Tracks your consistency across consecutive days.
- ⏱️ **Integrated Pomodoro Focus Timer**: 25m Focus, 50m Deep Work, and 5m Break modes with chime alert.
- 🎯 **Current Slot Detector**: Automatically highlights what slot you should be doing based on your system clock.
- ➕ **Custom Slot / Task Adder**: Easily add custom slots or ad-hoc todos.
- 📖 **Daily Learning Journal**: Auto-saves your daily breakthroughs and code notes.
- 🎓 **AI/ML Milestone Checklist**: 12 core curriculum milestones (SQL, Math, PyTorch, Transformers, RAG, FastAPI) to master over time.
- 💾 **Local Persistence & Backup**: Saved in your browser's local storage with single-click JSON Export & Import.
